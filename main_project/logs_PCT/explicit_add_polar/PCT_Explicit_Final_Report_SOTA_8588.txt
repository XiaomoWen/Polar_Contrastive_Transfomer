--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[3] ANALYSIS & DIAGNOSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Performance Ceiling (Saturation):
   The model reached the SOTA benchmark (85.88%) at Epoch 119. Subsequent training 
   (Ep 120-149) lowered the Training Loss but did not improve Test Accuracy. 
   This indicates "Model Capacity Saturation" for the current architecture.

2. Limitation of Explicit Polar Transform:
   - The current method applies geometric warping (Coordinate Transform) to input images.
   - Drawback: Pixel interpolation causes "Aliasing Effects" and loss of high-frequency 
     texture details, especially in the outer rings of the polar image.
   - This structural loss prevents the model from learning finer details required 
     to break the 86% accuracy barrier.

3. Scheduler Effectiveness:
   The Warmup + Cosine strategy proved highly effective. The stability of the final 
   epochs (fluctuating only between 85.73% - 85.88%) proves the model has converged 
   to a robust local minimum.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[4] STRATEGIC CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The potential of "Explicit Polar Transform + ViT" has been fully exhausted.
- Further hyperparameter tuning (LR, BatchSize) will likely only yield negligible 
  gains (< 0.1%) and is not cost-effective.
- To achieve a breakthrough (e.g., >87%), the fundamental approach to modeling 
  rotation must change.
- Direction shift recommended: From "Explicit Input Warping" to "Implicit Feature 
  Encoding" (e.g., Rotary Positional Embedding - RoPE).
