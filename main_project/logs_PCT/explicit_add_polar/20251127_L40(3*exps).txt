--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[PART 1] HISTORICAL EXPERIMENTS SUMMARY (DIAGNOSIS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

>> Round 1: The Baseline (Benchmark High)
-----------------------------------------
Config:     BatchSize=192 | LR=0.24 | Epochs=120 | Scheduler=StepLR
Result:     Sat->Drone R@1: 85.73%
Diagnosis:  
  - [Positive] Best performance so far. Best generalization.
  - [Negative] StepLR failed to lower LR significantly at the very end.
  - [Conclusion] BS=192 is the "Sweet Spot" for this dataset.

>> Round 2: The Underfit Case
-----------------------------------------
Config:     BatchSize=256 | LR=0.20 | Epochs=120 | Scheduler=StepLR
Result:     Sat->Drone R@1: 85.31% | Train Acc dropped to ~72%
Diagnosis:  
  - [Negative] Increased BS reduced total iterations significantly.
  - [Negative] LR=0.20 was too conservative for such a large batch.
  - [Conclusion] Model underfitting due to insufficient training steps.

>> Round 3: The Overfit Case
-----------------------------------------
Config:     BatchSize=256 | LR=0.32 | Epochs=180 | Scheduler=Warmup+Cosine
Result:     Sat->Drone R@1: 85.31% (Peak at Ep 159)
Diagnosis:  
  - [Positive] Training Loss reached historical low (0.21).
  - [Negative] Test accuracy plateaued/decreased despite low train loss.
  - [Conclusion] Linear Scaling Rule (LR=0.32) proved too aggressive for single-GPU, 
    leading to sharp minima and poor generalization. 180 Epochs was overkill.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[PART 2] CURRENT OPTIMIZATION STRATEGY (ROUND 4 - SOTA SPRINT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective:  Combine the stability of Round 1 (BS=192) with the superior convergence 
            of Round 3's Scheduler (Cosine).
Strategy:   A/B Testing on Learning Rate and Epochs.

>> Group A: Stability & Fine-Tuning (Conservative)
-----------------------------------------
Config:     BatchSize=192 | LR=0.22 | Epochs=150 | Scheduler=Warmup+Cosine
Hypothesis: Lower LR + Longer Decay = Flatter Minima (Better Generalization).

>> Group B: Replication & Extension (Aggressive)
-----------------------------------------
Config:     BatchSize=192 | LR=0.24 | Epochs=140 | Scheduler=Warmup+Cosine
Hypothesis: Replicates Round 1's best LR but fixes the "undercooked" ending 
            with Cosine decay and slight epoch increase.
